1) In one sentence, why put name, jersey, and poitns in one struct instead of three separate variables?
	To ensure that the data you want to access are all related.

2) What does p->jersey mean, and how is it different from p.jersey?
	p->jersey is a pointer to the memory location of the jersey variable in the struct.
	In contrast, p.jersey in a variable in the struct.

3) Why must init_player leave room for '\0' when copying into name[32]?
	Because leaving the '\0' will ensure that the char array name will act like
	a string instead of an array of characters.

4) Why does add_points take struct Player * instead of struct Player by value?
	Pointers to structures are better when passing into functions. This makes
	the backend more efficient becuase it avoids copying entire structures when
	being passed into functions

5) If you have struct Player roster[3], how do you pass the second player to print_player?
	print_player(roster[1]);
